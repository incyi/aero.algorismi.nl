# 🪂 No engine, no problem: I'm starting to glide at GoZC

{% include menu.html %}

Big news from the hangar: I'm going to start gliding at GoZC (Gooise Zweefvliegclub) in Hilversum, insallah! 🎉

## 🤔 Why gliding?

So far I flew with engines: the TL-3000, the Bristell and even the electric Velis. Then in May and June I did my introductory flights in the ASK 21 and the DG-1000S at EHLV and EHHV. Seven and nine minutes. Those minutes were enough. 😄

Gliding is flying in its purest form. No engine noise, just wind and the feeling that you are somehow sitting on top of the air. And the best part: **the sky is the fuel tank.** (My wallet is also happy about that. Mostly.)

## 🧠 What I'm excited (and a bit nervous) about

- **Thermals.** Finding invisible rising air and climbing in it like an elevator nobody told you about.
- **No go-around.** In a glider you get one landing. One. So the approach has to be good. Pressure? What pressure? 😅
- **Teamwork.** Gliding is a club sport. You don't just fly, you push, pull, tow, run with wings and drink coffee while waiting for your turn. I already love it.
- **Weather nerd mode ON.** Clouds are now not just clouds. They are information.

## 📘 The plan

1. Get registered and finish the paperwork at the club in December.
2. And start ground school immediately.
3. Start the lessons with an instructor in the two-seater around March.
4. Log everything in my new Glider logbook (yes, there will be a table, you know me).
5. Film as much as I can for my YouTube channel.
6. Eventually, by the end of 2027: my first solo. Insallah. 🤲

I'll write about every step here: the first lesson, the mistakes, the funny moments and the (hopefully) good landings.

Wish me tailwind, or better, lots of lift! 🛫

Blue skies,
— IY
